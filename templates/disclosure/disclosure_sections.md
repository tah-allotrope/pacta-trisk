## Governance (Quản trị)

The Board of Mekong Commercial Bank (MCB) holds ultimate oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities. The Risk Management Committee reviews transition-risk exposure across high-emitting sectors (power, cement, steel, automotive) at least semi-annually, and the Sustainable Finance & Climate Risk Office reports portfolio-alignment and transition-stress findings into the credit-risk governance cycle.

Management responsibility for assessing and managing climate-related risks sits with the Head of Sustainable Finance & Climate Risk, who integrates PACTA alignment and TRISK transition-stress outputs into sector strategy, credit appetite, and borrower engagement.

> **ISSB IFRS S2 cross-reference:** Governance disclosures (IFRS S2 paras 5–7) — the body responsible for oversight and management's role in monitoring climate-related risks and opportunities.
>
> **Decision 263/QĐ-TTg link:** Establishes the institutional accountability that underpins Vietnam's national green-growth and GHG-management objectives at the financial-institution level.

## Strategy (Chiến lược)

MCB assesses how climate-related transition risks affect its lending portfolio over short, medium, and long horizons. The forward-looking core is a comparison of financed production against Vietnam's Power Development Plan VIII (Quy hoạch điện VIII), the Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC), and an IEA Net-Zero (NZE) pathway.

Where the portfolio's financed technology mix diverges from these pathways, MCB treats the gap as a forward indicator of transition risk — both for the borrower (stranded-asset and margin risk) and for the bank (credit-quality migration). Strategy responses include sector exposure limits (giới hạn tín dụng theo ngành), sustainability-linked financing, and structured borrower engagement.

> **ISSB IFRS S2 cross-reference:** Strategy disclosures (IFRS S2 paras 9–23) — climate-related risks/opportunities, their effects on the business model and strategy, and climate resilience including scenario analysis.
>
> **Decision 263/QĐ-TTg link:** Supports the national transition toward low-carbon sectors by steering credit toward Paris-aligned activity.

## Risk Management (Quản lý rủi ro)

Climate transition risk is identified through PACTA alignment analysis and quantified through TRISK, which re-prices borrower assets and probability of default (xác suất vỡ nợ) under a late-and-sudden policy shock. Borrowers are ranked by a composite priority score combining alignment gap and transition-stress severity; the highest-priority names enter a structured engagement process.

These climate-risk processes are designed to feed MCB's overall credit-risk management — informing exposure reduction, engagement prioritisation, and sector limits — rather than operating as a standalone exercise.

> **ISSB IFRS S2 cross-reference:** Risk Management disclosures (IFRS S2 paras 24–26) — processes to identify, assess, prioritise, and monitor climate-related risks, and their integration into overall risk management.
>
> **Decision 263/QĐ-TTg link:** Aligns with the expectation that credit institutions identify and manage climate and environmental risk within their risk frameworks.

## Metrics & Targets (Chỉ số và Mục tiêu)

MCB monitors: (i) financed technology-mix alignment versus PDP8/NDC/NZE pathways by sector; (ii) emission-intensity gaps for cement and steel via the Sectoral Decarbonisation Approach (SDA); and (iii) TRISK net-present-value and probability-of-default changes under stress for covered borrowers.

The bank's working target is to narrow the financed-alignment gap in priority sectors over successive reporting cycles, supported by borrower transition plans and sustainability-linked instruments. Targets shown in any specific cycle are illustrative for this demonstration and would be calibrated against the bank's risk appetite and national policy in a production deployment.

> **ISSB IFRS S2 cross-reference:** Metrics & Targets disclosures (IFRS S2 paras 27–37) — cross-industry metrics, GHG emissions (including financed emissions, Category 15), and targets used to manage climate-related risks.
>
> **Decision 263/QĐ-TTg link:** Provides quantitative evidence for Vietnam's GHG-management and green-growth monitoring expectations at portfolio level.
