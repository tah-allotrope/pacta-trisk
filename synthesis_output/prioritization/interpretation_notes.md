## Interpretation

**Power remains the clear priority sector** for BIDV's climate risk
management, scoring 0.863 (Critical band) on the absolute composite scale.
This is driven by three converging factors:

1. **Alignment gap:** Power's coal capacity is 14.4 percentage points above
   the PDP8 target — an alignment severity of 0.610 on Table A1's
   0–40 pp scale. The portfolio is over-allocated to coal relative to the
   PDP8 pathway.

2. **Transition stress:** The power sector's TRISK stress-test results
   (including three coal-heavy entities with the largest NPV losses under
   stress) produce the maximum stress severity (1.000 on Table B).

3. **Portfolio exposure:** Power accounts for 81.8% of total Decision
   263-relevant exposure, itself saturating Table C's exposure-severity
   scale at 1.000 (breakpoints top out at a 50% share).

**Steel now ranks second** (0.646, High), not last. Its SDA alignment gap
(7.2% of the 2030 target) is the *most severe of the three sectors* on
Table A2's absolute scale (severity 0.610, tied with power's raw severity
despite the different unit), and its stress severity (0.961) is
near-maximal. Its exposure share (7.8%) is the smallest of the three,
which is what keeps its composite below power's.

**Cement ranks third** (0.556, High — not "Low"). Its alignment gap (2.1%)
is genuinely the smallest of the three sectors (severity 0.258), but its
stress severity reaches the table maximum (1.000), which is enough on its
own to place cement in the High band even before its below-average
exposure share (10.4%, severity 0.384) is weighted in.

**The headline change from the pre-correction version of this document:**
every sector now reads High or Critical — there is no "Low" sector in the
corrected portfolio. A prioritization deliverable that told BIDV cement and
steel were low-priority was wrong under the platform's own current
methodology; this file existed to be copy-pasted into a client-facing
report, which makes the correction load-bearing, not cosmetic.

## Caveats

- **Synthetic portfolio:** All results are based on the synthetic MCB
  portfolio, not real BIDV data. BIDV-specific results will differ based on
  actual sector exposure and borrower composition.
- **Different alignment units, same table family, different tables:**
  Power/automotive gaps are in percentage points of market share (Table A1)
  while cement/steel gaps are in percent of target emission intensity
  (Table A2) — two different anchor tables are used for exactly this
  reason; they are never pooled into one min-max ranking.
