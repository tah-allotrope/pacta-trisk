01 / Executive Summary

Executive Summary

What Was Built

A 15-slide prospect pitch deck (present/bank_prospect_deck.pptx) that walks a prospective Vietnamese commercial bank from the Decision 263 transition-risk problem through PACTA portfolio alignment, TRISK borrower stress testing, a synthetic Vietnam-bank case study, and a four-phase engagement offer — all using the Allotrope brand template.

Why It Matters

The repo held abundant analytical output (PACTA/TRISK charts, HTML reports, a live Streamlit dashboard) but zero slides. This deck closes that gap, giving the team a tangible leave-behind for prospect meetings. The scripted build (build_deck.py) makes the deck reproducible and iterable — text or chart changes can be re-applied in seconds.

02 / Background & Objective

Background & Objective

The pacta-trisk repository contains a complete PACTA (Paris Agreement Capital Transition Assessment) and TRISK (Transition Risk Impact on Stress and Knowledge) analytical pipeline for a synthetic Vietnamese commercial bank. Over six months of work produced portfolio alignment results, borrower-level stress tests, a 7-page Streamlit dashboard, engagement letters, TCFD/ISSB disclosure packs, and multiple HTML reports.

Despite this richness, the repo had no prospect-facing slide artifact. The only deck was a 25-slide internal funder-update (present/ref/Allotrope GTB updates_Tara Vietnam_December 2025.pptx) covering BESS case studies and BIDV/TCB status tracking — content irrelevant to a prospect pitch.

The objective was to produce a polished .pptx that is visually indistinguishable from the Allotrope reference deck (same masters, layouts, logos, 16:9 geometry) but re-narrated as a capability pitch to a prospective bank, walking from transition-risk problem to engagement offer.

05 / Methodology

Methodology

The build followed a five-phase sequential pipeline:

PHASE-01 (Foundation): Created a working copy of the reference deck, ran inspect_template.py to produce a 280-line shape-index map, and wrote a 198-line slide-by-slide narrative outline mapping all 25 reference slides to keep/repurpose/drop decisions with named assets for each image slot.

PHASE-02 (Assets): Gathered 13 PACTA/TRISK chart PNGs from existing repo outputs into present/assets/, matching the outline's image slot references.

PHASE-03 (Build): Wrote present/build_deck.py (280 lines) using python-pptx to drop 10 internal-status slides, apply 87 text replacements and 8 image replacements across 15 slides, then rendered to PNGs via LibreOffice + PyMuPDF for visual QA.

PHASE-04 (Polish): Wrote present/add_speaker_notes.py (160 lines) to add detailed presenter guidance (261–579 chars per slide) covering welcome, methodology, case-study interpretation, engagement offer, and Q&A preparation. Verified contact and compliance slides.

PHASE-05 (Ship): Marked the plan as completed, updated activeContext.md, and pushed all artifacts to main.

07 / Findings & Recommendation

Findings & Recommendation

The scripted approach works. The build_deck.py script runs cleanly (exit 0), produces a 15-slide deck from the 25-slide reference, and applies all text and image replacements as specified in the outline. The deck opens correctly in PowerPoint and Google Slides with full brand fidelity.

Speaker notes add significant value. The 7,620 characters of presenter guidance across 15 slides transform the deck from a static leave-behind into a presentation-ready tool. Notes include audience-engagement prompts ("How is your bank currently assessing transition risk?"), methodology explanations, and Q&A preparation.

Image replacement requires private API access. python-pptx does not expose a public setter for shape.image.blob. The workaround (shape.image._blob) works but may break in future python-pptx versions. This is the single largest technical risk for long-term maintainability.

Recommendation: Use the deck as-is for the next prospect meeting. Before the meeting, capture fresh dashboard screenshots from pactavn.streamlit.app for slide 11. Run through the full deck with speaker notes to verify timing (target: 20–25 minutes including Q&A).

09 / Risks / Open Questions

Risks / Open Questions

Risks

  • python-pptx private API: shape.image._blob may break in future versions. Mitigation: pin python-pptx version in requirements.txt; monitor release notes.
  • LibreOffice rendering gap: 2 of 15 slides fail to render due to complex grouped shapes. Mitigation: acceptable for QA; the PPTX itself is complete.
  • Synthetic data perception: Prospects may misread synthetic results as real portfolio data. Mitigation: disclaimer on every data slide; verbal framing in speaker notes.

Open Questions

  • Should the deck be parameterized for different prospects (e.g., Techcombank vs. a new bank)?
  • Should a Vietnamese-language variant be produced for local bank meetings?
  • Should the deck include a "track record" slide with real engagement results (post-pilot)?
  • Should the build script be integrated into a CI/CD pipeline for automated deck regeneration when charts update?
