Executive Summary

What was wrong, what shipped, and the proof that it worked.

Wave 1 made the pipeline reproducible: cross-artifact invariants run in CI and a byte-identity checker proves a run reproduces its committed outputs. Wave 2 attacked the three defects that made the platform's output wrong rather than merely incomplete.

1 · The money was on the wrong scale

The synthetic MCB loanbook was denominated in millions of VND while every column, label, and document said VND. The public demo portfolio therefore read as USD 950 instead of the ~USD 950M its own pitch deck claims. A 1,000,000× rescale at the source (make_loan()) put every engagement on the same whole-VND scale, guarded by a new invariant (INV-006) that fails if any VND-declared loanbook is implausibly small.

2 · The scores were rank-relative

Priority scores were min-max normalized — up to three times over the same quantity — so the top-ranked sector or borrower was always exactly 1.0 and two structurally different banks produced identical score patterns. Anchored absolute severity tables (docs/scoring_anchors.md) replaced all three layers, so a score means the same thing across sectors, banks, and refreshes.

3 · The intake deleted client exposure

The intake validator rejected the sector codes and currencies its own published schema advertised (D3510 power rows, USD rows), and errored rows vanished from normalized_loanbook.csv with nothing reconciling submitted money against processed money. The two-tier error/warning model, widened sector map, and one-at-intake FX conversion closed the gap; the coverage report makes every retained/dropped/NA row visible in VND.

4 · The acceptance checks were not where we said they were

CLAUDE.md claimed both byte-identity and the invariants ran in CI on every push. Only the invariants did. The wave's first phase made the documents true: byte-identity now runs on every push and gates the weekly auto-publish of the public snapshot, with an allow_drift dispatch input as the documented escape hatch for a reviewed refreeze.

Phase-by-Phase Analysis

All six phases of the wave, in dependency order.

Phase 1 — Byte-identity gate in CI and on the weekly publish DONE

A new byte-identity job in ci.yml runs tools/verify_refactor.R on every push; refresh.yml classifies the working tree (--skip-refresh) before it commits the public snapshot, overridable only via allow_drift: true. CLAUDE.md law 5 and README.md were corrected to match. The negative-control (append a line, expect drift + exit 1) was proven in the phase commit.

  • Byte-identity runs in ci.yml on every push.
  • Drift gate inserted in refresh.yml before the commit step.
  • allow_drift workflow-dispatch input documented as the escape hatch.

Phase 2 — True VND everywhere, one money formatter, one new invariant DONE

R/format_money.R provides format_vnd_full(), format_vnd_bn(), and vnd_to_billion(). The three ad-hoc formatters (two of which printed a misleading "(synthetic units)") were deleted. make_loan() multiplies by the single constant VND_PER_MILLION = 1e6. INV-006 (median VND-loan plausibility) joined the invariant suite. Prose in the pitch-deck source, the README, methodology docs, and interpretation notes was corrected to ~USD 950M.

Ratio-invariance verified (ASM-002): rescaling every loan by the same constant changed no PACTA market-share or SDA intensity output — the committed ratio-based files were byte-identical after the rescale.

Phase 3 — Absolute severity scores replacing min-max normalization DONE

R/severity_scoring.R implements clamped piecewise-linear severity against four documented anchor tables (two alignment bases, TRISK loss, exposure concentration) in docs/scoring_anchors.md. normalise_01() and the two min-max blocks were deleted; the dashboard's stress_priority_score sort key was deliberately preserved (ASM-004) while a new absolute stress_severity_score was added alongside it. A latent NA-propagation bug in prioritize_sectors() was fixed with an explicit stop() naming unmapped sectors. New unit tests (severity functions + prioritize_sectors() — previously one of 27 untested functions) and a degeneracy test with a single-sector config lock the behavior in.

  • No normalise_01 left; no rescale() in prioritization_core.R.
  • No composite_score equals exactly 0.0 or 1.0 in any committed artifact.
  • MCB vs SDB top scores now differ by more than 0.01 (golden assertion).

Phase 4 — Single golden refreeze to 0.4.0 DONE

One reviewable refreeze commit regenerated every artifact affected by Phases 2–3, re-pinned the golden tests to falsifiable values, added the non-degeneracy guard, and bumped DESCRIPTION/NEWS.md to 0.4.0. The scenario grid parquet stayed untouched (grid_contract_version "v2", fingerprint unchanged), and the TRISK grid was not regenerated (ASM-005).

Phase 5 — Intake honors its published contract DONE

scripts/intake_validate_and_map.R now uses a two-tier outcome model: hard errors (missing name, non-numeric/negative money, unparseable sector code, duplicates) drop a row; every condition the schema describes as a classification is a warning that retains the row, written to validation_warnings.csv. The six-entry code allow-list became one table of 20 accepted codes covering ISIC Rev.4 parents and VSIC 2018 sub-classes (D3510 → power, etc.); zero-padding only ever pads codes shorter than 4 digits. USD rows are converted once at intake via the new inputs.fx_rate_usd_vnd key; without a rate they are retained with exposure NA and the intake exits non-zero naming the missing key — never silently dropped, never guessed.

Proven on the SDB fixture: 34 normalized rows (up from 24), the seven previously-rejected D3510 power rows retained and mapped to power, both USD rows converted at 26,300 VND/USD, and validation_warnings.csv written on every run — even when empty.

Phase 6 — Coverage and reconciliation report DONE

scripts/generate_coverage_report.R produces a self-contained HTML report plus a machine-readable, tracked coverage_metrics.json per engagement that answers — in both row counts and VND — what the client sent, what was normalized, what was dropped and why, what was retained-with-warning and why, and what fraction of processed exposure has ABCD asset-level coverage. Added as a coverage_report orchestrator step immediately after validation_report (inside the intake branch). SDB's coverage_metrics.json and validation_warnings.csv are tracked fixtures; the HTML itself stays gitignored (timestamp-bearing).

Risks, Caveats & Open Questions

Anchor breakpoints are a documented judgment call. The severity tables in docs/scoring_anchors.md have no external standard to copy. They are deliberately reviewable as data — a reader with a calculator can recompute MCB power's alignment_score of 0.60975 from alignment_gap_raw = 14.39. Disagreement about thresholds is a productive conversation; disagreement about whether "1.0" means anything is not.
Widening the intake admit-list lowers headline match rates over an honest denominator. Retained out-of-scope and newly-admitted rows increase the classified population that PACTA cannot match to ABCD. This is correct: the coverage report exists precisely to make it legible — a lower match rate over a larger, honest denominator is the right number.
The USD conversion rate has no authoritative in-repo source. inputs.fx_rate_usd_vnd is operator-configured per engagement and recorded in the pipeline manifest. The SDB fixture uses 26,300 VND/USD. For a real engagement, agree the rate (e.g. SBV reference rate) before intake.
Intake rows retained with NA exposure are visible, not processed. A USD row without a configured rate (or any unsupported currency) stays in the normalized loanbook with exposure NA and a warning, and the intake exits non-zero. It contributes 0 VND to every money total and the coverage report states this explicitly.

Deliberately out of scope (future waves)

  • Real bank data; TRISK beyond the current sectors.
  • Making the Streamlit dashboard intake-warning/coverage aware (a natural consumer of coverage_metrics.json).
  • The executive-summary generator that coverage_metrics.json was designed to feed.
  • R CMD check cleanup and retiring dead scripts.
