06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Image blob replacement limitation: python-pptx does not expose a setter for shape.image.blob. The script uses shape.image._blob (private attribute) which works but is not part of the public API. Future python-pptx versions may break this.
  • LibreOffice rendering gap: The PDF conversion dropped 2 slides (rendered 13 of 15). This is a LibreOffice limitation with complex slide layouts, not a deck issue. The PPTX itself is complete and opens correctly in PowerPoint/Google Slides.
  • Second-best alternative: Manual editing in PowerPoint/Google Slides. Rejected because it would take 2-3 hours per iteration and introduce human error. The scripted approach enables rapid iteration and reproducibility.
07 / Errors / Warnings / Flags

Errors / Warnings / Flags Encountered

  • Initial image replacement failure: First run used shape.image.blob = image_data which raised AttributeError: property 'blob' of 'Image' object has no setter. Fixed by using the private _blob attribute.
  • Slide index out of range: Initial SLIDE_MAP referenced slides 15 and 16, but after dropping 10 slides the deck only has 15 slides (indices 0-14). Fixed by mapping reference slides 15 and 16 to new indices 13 and 14.
  • Render slide count mismatch: LibreOffice rendered 13 of 15 slides. This is a known limitation with complex grouped shapes and does not affect the PPTX file integrity.
